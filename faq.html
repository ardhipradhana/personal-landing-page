How quickly can we start seeing results?⌄
Most clients see actionable insights within the first week and measurable improvements within 30 days. We prioritize 2–3 quick wins in the first month.
What does the first month look like?⌄
Week 1: discovery and diagnostics. Week 2–3: cash clarity, margin levers, and process fixes. Week 4: 90‑day roadmap and KPI setup.
How is this different from hiring a full-time CFO?⌄
You get senior-level expertise without full-time overhead. I bring cross‑industry frameworks and implement with your team at a fraction of the cost.
Which types of businesses do you work with?⌄
SaaS, retail, education, and service businesses. Typical revenue range: ~$500K to $50M+. The approach adapts to your stage and complexity.
Do you work with companies outside Indonesia?⌄
Yes. I work with international clients remotely and accommodate time zones. Financial principles and models are globally applicable.
What’s included in the Strategic CFO Partnership?⌄
Weekly strategic sessions, reporting and analysis, cash flow planning, budget/forecast, investor support, and advisory for major decisions.
How do we collaborate day-to-day?⌄
Weekly sessions, async updates, shared dashboards, and on-call support for critical decisions. I integrate with your existing tools.
How do I know which service is right for me?⌄
Start with a free Discovery Conversation. We’ll map your goals and constraints, then choose the smallest intervention that creates the biggest change.
What if I’m not satisfied?⌄
If you’re not satisfied within the first 30 minutes of the first session, you pay nothing. For ongoing work, we can adjust scope or part ways with notice.
